### Mobile Lead Drivers
- **Floating WhatsApp Button**: Pinned to bottom-right (`bottom: 80px`), pre-filled message for instant enquiry.
- **Sticky Bottom Action Bar**: Fixed at bottom for quick phone calling and dealer application.
- **Touch Inputs**: All form fields set to minimum `48px` height to ensure effortless touch interaction.
