# Pawnourish Website Requirements

## Project Overview

Pawnourish is a **B2B wholesale supplier** specializing in the distribution of **Royal Canin** and **Drools** pet food products.

The objective of this project is to design and develop a premium, conversion-focused **portfolio website** that establishes Pawnourish as a trusted distribution partner for pet retailers.

This is **not an eCommerce website**. Products are displayed to showcase the company's offerings and capabilities—not for direct online purchases.

---

# Primary Business Goal

The website's primary objective is to **generate qualified B2B enquiries** from:

* Pet Stores
* Veterinary Clinics
* Pet Pharmacies
* Pet Grooming Stores
* Pet Product Distributors
* Breeders

with a primary focus on the **Delhi NCR** region.

Every page, section, and interaction should encourage visitors to take the next step toward becoming a retail partner.

# Website Objectives

The website should:

* Build trust and credibility.
* Present Pawnourish as a reliable wholesale supplier.
* Showcase the Royal Canin and Drools product portfolio.
* Highlight the advantages of partnering with Pawnourish.
* Encourage retailers to enquire about dealership opportunities.
* Drive WhatsApp conversations, phone calls, and enquiry form submissions.
* Generate high-quality B2B leads from the Delhi NCR market.

---

# Lead Generation Strategy

The website should prioritize lead generation over product browsing.

Recommended conversion elements include:

* "Become a Dealer" CTA
* "Request Wholesale Pricing" CTA
* WhatsApp Chat Button
* Click-to-Call Button
* Dealer Enquiry Form
* Quick Callback Request
* Product Catalogue Request
* Sticky Mobile CTA
* Multiple contact opportunities throughout the page

Any feature or interaction that improves enquiry generation, reduces friction, or increases conversion rates is encouraged.

---

# Brand Positioning

Pawnourish should be positioned as:

* A trusted wholesale distribution partner
* A reliable supplier of genuine products
* A long-term business partner for retailers
* A professional B2B organization with dependable service and inventory

The website should communicate confidence, reliability, and professionalism rather than focusing on retail shopping.

# Visual Direction

The website should feel:

* Premium
* Modern
* Clean
* Enterprise
* Professional
* Trustworthy
* Spacious

Avoid:

* Cartoon illustrations
* Overly playful pet-themed designs
* Busy layouts
* Retail-focused shopping aesthetics

The overall design should reflect the professionalism of the Pawnourish letterhead and use its teal-to-green color palette with generous whitespace, rounded corners, subtle shadows, and clean typography.

* Generate more qualified retailer enquiries.
* Increase WhatsApp conversations and phone enquiries.

---

# Design Principle

Every section should answer one simple question:

**"Does this help convert a visitor into a retailer enquiry?"**

If a section does not contribute to building trust, educating potential partners, or encouraging them to contact Pawnourish, it should be redesigned or removed. The website should be optimized for conversions first, aesthetics second.
